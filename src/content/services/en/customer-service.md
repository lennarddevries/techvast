## What I do for you

Customers expect quick responses and clear answers. I keep your communication channels professional and responsive at all times.

### Email support
- Answering customer enquiries within agreed SLA
- Handling and escalating complaints as needed
- Sending follow-up emails
- Writing templates for recurring questions

### Live chat & ticketing
- Managing Intercom, Zendesk or Freshdesk
- Triaging and assigning tickets
- Building and maintaining FAQ database
- Monitoring CSAT scores

### Onboarding support
- Guiding new clients through onboarding
- Sending welcome emails and guides
- Scheduling check-in calls
- Identifying churn signals early

## How I work

I work from within your tools and follow your tone of voice. After a brief 1–2 week onboarding period I communicate independently with your customers.

**Minimum commitment:** 10 hours per month
