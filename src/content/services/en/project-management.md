## What I do for you

Projects rarely fail because of bad ideas — they stall due to poor follow-through. I hold the reins so your team can focus on execution.

### Project planning
- Defining and documenting project scope
- Building timelines and milestones
- Task distribution and accountability
- Risk identification

### Task tracking
- Maintaining action lists (Jira, Trello, Notion, Asana)
- Monitoring deadlines and escalating when needed
- Identifying and communicating blockers
- Supporting sprint administration

### Stakeholder communication
- Weekly progress reports
- Meeting minutes and decision logs
- Status updates for management
- Keeping documentation current

### Process improvement
- Identifying bottlenecks
- Proposing workflow optimisations
- Writing SOPs (standard operating procedures)
