## What I do for you

Social media visibility is increasingly important for IT companies and startups. I ensure consistency and a professional presence.

### Content planning
- Creating monthly content calendars
- Aligning topics with your objectives
- Integrating seasonal and topical content
- Defining content pillars

### Content creation & scheduling
- Writing captions in your tone of voice
- Scheduling posts via Buffer, Later or Hootsuite
- Preparing visuals (Canva templates)
- Hashtag strategy

### Community management
- Responding to comments and DMs
- Monitoring and joining relevant conversations
- Flagging and escalating negative posts
- Tracking followers and engagement

### Reporting
- Monthly analytics report
- Growth figures and engagement metrics
- Recommendations for improvement

## How I work

I start with a thorough audit of your existing channels and strategy. Based on that I build a content plan which we review together monthly.

**Platforms:** LinkedIn, Instagram, X (Twitter), Facebook
