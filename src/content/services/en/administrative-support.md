## What I do for you

Administrative tasks are indispensable, but they eat away hours you could spend better. With my experience in IT environments and corporate administration, I elevate your back-office to the next level.

- Setting up filters, labels and auto-responses
- Inbox zero: structural clean-up actions

### Calendar management
- Scheduling and confirming appointments
- Identifying and resolving conflicts
- Setting up recurring tasks and reminders
- Coordination with external parties

### Document management
- Digital archiving and naming conventions
- Structuring Google Drive / SharePoint / OneDrive
- Creating templates for frequently used documents
- Version control and access management

### Data entry
- Updating and cleaning CRM data
- Populating Excel reports and dashboards
- Processing invoices and receipts
